Tarot Deck — Android use and purchase information

Platform use and purchase information · Updated 5 November 2026. This notice documents existing platform handling; it does not introduce additional contractual obligations.

Android preparation currently has no configured Google Play products and no purchasable subscription. The existing iOS offers are not an Android catalog. No Android prices, product IDs or release date are announced here.

The intended optional monthly Google Play subscription removes advertising; reading, learning, card reference, favorites and custom-spread editing remain free. There is no verified active Android offer yet. Before any offer becomes purchasable, Google Play must show its actual local price, billing period and renewal conditions before confirmation. This page does not set a new price or activate an offer.

For a subscription purchased through Google Play, use Google Play subscriptions to manage or cancel. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. Restore Purchases rechecks Play entitlements when configured; do not assume an Apple subscription is transferable to Android. Refunds follow Google Play’s applicable refund process. The developer does not receive payment-card details. For help contact coderappskrazel@gmail.com.
