Tarot Deck — Android use and purchase information

Updated 5 November 2026 · Actualizado el 5 de noviembre de 2026.

Purchases are currently unavailable in the prepared Android app. When offered, an optional monthly subscription removes ads; all offered amounts provide the same benefit. Tarot reading, learning, favorites and custom spreads remain free.

Google Play shows the localized price, currency and monthly renewal conditions before confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. Manage or cancel through Google Play subscriptions; uninstalling does not cancel. Cancellation normally stops the next renewal, without ending an already-paid period. Restore Purchases refreshes Play entitlements when available. Refunds follow Google Play’s applicable policy.
