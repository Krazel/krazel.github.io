Ad-supported iOS versions

Remove Ads is an optional one-time purchase that removes banners and interstitials; it does not unlock gameplay advantages. To recover an existing purchase, open Remove Ads → Restore Purchases using the same Apple account. Available ad privacy choices can be reopened from Remove Ads. Purchases and ads need a connection; the game can be played without buying.

Versiones iOS con anuncios

Quitar anuncios es una compra única opcional que elimina banners e intersticiales; no desbloquea ventajas de juego. Para recuperar una compra, abre Quitar anuncios → Restaurar compras con la misma cuenta de Apple. Las opciones de privacidad publicitaria disponibles pueden reabrirse desde Quitar anuncios. Las compras y los anuncios requieren conexión; puedes jugar sin comprar.

Tilt Arena Support

Tilt Arena is an offline iPhone arcade game. Calibrate your posture, tilt the device to guide the arrow, dodge enemies, collect orbs and pause with the in-game button.

Controls

  • Hold the iPhone in landscape and use the calibration posture shown in the game.
  • Tilt the device to move the arrow through the arena.
  • Touch an orb with the arrow to collect it and use the resulting power at the right moment.
  • Tap the pause button to stop the run. Resume without recalibrating.

Tilt Arena es un juego arcade para iPhone que funciona sin conexión. Calibra la postura, inclina el dispositivo para guiar la flecha, esquiva enemigos, recoge orbes y pausa la partida con el botón.

Android support / Soporte Android

Added 5 November 2026 · Añadido el 5 de noviembre de 2026.

The Android editions use local game resources and local saves. Android preparation requires Android 8.0 or later and a functioning Android System WebView. This notice does not announce a public release or prove that your device has been tested.

An accelerometer and landscape orientation are required. Tilt to steer and tap to pause. Normal and Inclined keep their fixed reference; custom calibration stores your chosen angle. Use the game’s posture and recalibration settings.

Try closing and reopening the app first. Clearing data or reinstalling can remove local saves. For help, send only the app version, Android version and a short description to coderappskrazel@gmail.com.

Android use and purchase information / Información de uso y compras Android

Android 1.1.5 adds an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase; all gameplay stays free. Use Restore Purchases with the purchasing Google Account to recover access. A cancelled or pending purchase does not grant ownership; unresolved store access blocks new ads while the game stays playable. Restoration does not recover deleted local scores. The current Play test build has commercial ads disabled; a separate demonstration APK is for test ads only. Age and privacy choices are available through settings in advertising editions. Read the Android privacy notice for Google Billing diagnostics and the planned ad/consent processing. Do not send full receipts, purchase tokens, passwords or card details to support.
