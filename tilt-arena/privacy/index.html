Tilt Arena Privacy Policy

Updated: November 21, 2026 · iOS versions 1.1.5 and later, including TestFlight

Tilt Arena is published by Krazel Games. The ad-supported iPhone edition includes an optional one-time Apple purchase that removes banners and interstitial advertisements. The original iOS edition and Android notice are retained below.

Game information stays on your device

Motion readings steer the arrow on your device. Calibration, settings, scores, first-opening notice history and the time of the last interstitial are stored locally. The game does not upload motion samples, calibration or your score history to Krazel, and does not create a game account. Uninstalling removes local preferences.

Advertising and privacy choices

Ad-supported versions use Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. Google may process IP-derived approximate location, app- or device-bounded identifiers and advertising identifiers when platform permission allows, advertisements and interactions, crash information and performance diagnostics for advertising, measurement and analytics. These SDK requests are separate from the local game information described above. There are no additional mediation networks in this version.

Before requesting advertisements, the app updates applicable privacy choices and checks whether the consent SDK permits requests. Available ad privacy choices can be reopened from Remove Ads. Known child and teen settings are preserved; an unspecified age is not classified as an adult. Ad content is limited to general-audience content for minors and unspecified ages. This version does not request Apple's IDFA tracking permission or force ad personalization. Google must respect the available system permissions and applicable privacy signals. Refusing a privacy choice does not prevent playing the game.

Google handles its advertising data under its own retention and account controls. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google ad controls. Krazel does not receive your advertising profile.

Purchases and ad-free access

Apple handles payment and transaction records. Tilt Arena verifies signed StoreKit purchase rights and the original app acquisition on the device to provide and restore ad-free access. Krazel does not receive your payment-card information. Verified ad-free users do not start or request ads. Restore Purchases is available from the purchase screen. Original-version users retain ad-free access under the transition to the free edition.

Support, website and contact

If you email coderappskrazel@gmail.com, Gmail/Google processes the address, message and information you voluntarily supply to answer your request. You may request deletion of that correspondence. Do not send passwords or sensitive identifiers. Public help and privacy pages are hosted by GitHub under GitHub's privacy notice. Tilt Arena support.

Tilt Arena Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Data collection

The app does not collect personal data, create user accounts, track you, show advertising, use analytics, or send gameplay or motion data to the developer or to third parties. The app has no network service or in-app purchase flow.

Android 1.1.5 preparation

This notice covers Android 1.1.5 (1001501), prepared for testing and Google Play purchase setup. The delivered Play build keeps commercial ads disabled pending consent configuration, policy verification and further testing. A separate demonstration APK uses Google test ads. This page does not announce public availability; the iOS sections above remain specific to Apple.

Scores, control posture, calibration, settings, the age band, notice preferences and ad-timing state are stored locally. The entered numeric age is not retained. The game has no account or Krazel gameplay server and does not send your scores or control-sensor samples to Krazel. Android app-data backup is disabled. Clearing data or uninstalling can erase local progress and settings.

Google Play handles the optional one-time purchase that removes banners and interstitials, without a gameplay advantage or subscription. The app receives product, purchase-state, token and signed receipt information, verifies ownership, acknowledges completed purchases and queries Play again for restoration or updated ownership. Krazel has no payment form or purchase server and does not receive card details. Google retains its store records separately from local saves.

Google Play Billing 9.1 reports billing-operation and error diagnostics to Google through DataTransport, which can queue reports locally. Reports include app/library versions, billing-session information and technical device, Android-build, language/region, time-zone and network/operator metadata. These SDK reports are separate from gameplay saves; deleting the game does not control Google's retention.

The integrated Google Mobile Ads 25.5 and UMP 4 libraries support advertising and consent. When their request paths are enabled, Google processes IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), permitted device/account identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostic/performance information for ads, measurement and fraud prevention. UMP processes privacy choices and app/device/region information. Google's policies and the providers shown in its consent interface apply. No mediation adapter or independent Krazel analytics service was added.

Before advertising, a neutral age choice configures the Google age-treatment signals; only the band is saved locally. Unknown age and users under 16 receive child/under-consent treatment with child-appropriate content. Treating ages 13–15 this way is a conservative setting even where the local consent age is lower. Ages 16–17 use teen treatment; adults have no additional app-wide content cap. Google may impose stricter account/device protections. The age choice can be revisited in settings.

Advertising editions respect the actual consent choice and Google's regional requirements. You can accept, refuse and revisit privacy choices where offered. Changing age or privacy choices discards cached ads while the current state is refreshed. Errors or unresolved purchase/consent state block new ads; the game remains playable. No extra non-personalized-ad setting is forced for every user. Store, consent and ad processing use Google services and are not all local-only operations.

For optional support, email coderappskrazel@gmail.com. We use your address and message to handle the request and keep them only as needed for that purpose; Gmail hosts the mailbox. You can request deletion of that correspondence at the same address. Do not send passwords, card details or purchase tokens. GitHub hosts these public pages and may process technical web-request information. For Google-controlled purchase, diagnostic and ad records, use Google's privacy controls; clearing local game data does not erase those records. See the Google Privacy Policy and GitHub privacy notice.
