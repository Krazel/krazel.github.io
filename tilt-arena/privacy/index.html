Tilt Arena Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Tilt Arena is an iPhone game designed to work locally on your device. This policy explains what the app stores and how that information is handled.

Data collection

The app does not collect personal data, create user accounts, track you, show advertising, use analytics, or send gameplay or motion data to the developer or to third parties. The app has no network service or in-app purchase flow.

Information stored on your device

Your selected control posture, calibration, and personal high score may be stored locally so the app can restore your choices and progress. This information is not uploaded by the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data.

Apple services

Apple may process App Store distribution and TestFlight information under Apple's own policies. The app itself does not send gameplay, motion, calibration, or score data to Krazel or another service.

Support email

If you choose to email support, Gmail/Google processes your email address, message, and any iOS version you voluntarily include so the developer can reply and troubleshoot. Do not send passwords, device identifiers, or other sensitive information.

Contact

For privacy questions, email coderappskrazel@gmail.com or use the Tilt Arena support page.

Política de privacidad de Tilt Arena

Última actualización: 15 de septiembre de 2026

Tilt Arena es un juego para iPhone diseñado para funcionar localmente en tu dispositivo. Esta política explica qué guarda la app y cómo se trata esa información.

Recopilación de datos

La app no recopila datos personales, no crea cuentas, no te rastrea, no muestra publicidad, no utiliza analítica ni envía datos de juego o movimiento al desarrollador o a terceros. La app no tiene servicio de red ni compras integradas.

Información guardada en el dispositivo

La postura de control elegida, la calibración y tu récord personal pueden guardarse localmente para restaurar tus opciones y progreso. La app no sube esta información. Al eliminar la app se borran sus datos locales.

Servicios de Apple

Apple puede tratar la información de distribución en App Store y TestFlight según sus propias políticas. La app no envía datos de juego, movimiento, calibración ni puntuación a Krazel ni a otro servicio.

Correo de soporte

Si decides escribir a soporte, Gmail/Google trata tu dirección, el mensaje y la versión de iOS que incluyas voluntariamente para que el desarrollador pueda responder y resolver el problema. No envíes contraseñas, identificadores del dispositivo ni otra información sensible.

Contacto

Para consultas de privacidad, escribe a coderappskrazel@gmail.com o utiliza la página de soporte de Tilt Arena.

Android privacy / Privacidad Android

Added 5 November 2026 · Añadido el 5 de noviembre de 2026.

This additional notice covers Tilt Arena for Android. The iOS sections above remain specific to the Apple edition.

Game progress, records where present and preferences are kept in local app storage; the game does not create an app account or send this gameplay data to a Krazel server. These premium editions contain no advertising, advertising SDK, independent analytics service or in-app purchase flow.

Motion-sensor readings steer the arrow on the device. The selected posture, custom calibration, records and settings are stored locally; the game does not upload sensor samples or calibration. The Android configuration disables automatic app-data backup.

Local settings and scores remain until you clear app data or uninstall the Android app.

Google Play handles Android distribution and any paid-download transaction under its policies; the game does not receive payment-card details. Opening public help pages or sending email uses external services: GitHub hosts these pages, and Gmail hosts the support mailbox. If you contact support, your email and message are used to handle the request; you can request deletion of that correspondence at coderappskrazel@gmail.com. Do not send credentials or device identifiers. See Google Privacy Policy / Política de privacidad de Google and GitHub privacy notice / aviso de privacidad de GitHub.
