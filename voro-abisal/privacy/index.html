VORO: Abyssal — Privacy

VORO: Abyssal is designed to play without an account and without automatically sending personal data to our servers.

Data stored on your device

Progress and preferences are stored locally on the device. You can delete them from the game's settings.

Data we do not collect

The app does not use advertising or tracking and does not create user profiles. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties.

Contact

VORO: Abisal está diseñado para jugar sin cuenta y sin enviar automáticamente datos personales a nuestros servidores.

Datos guardados en tu dispositivo
