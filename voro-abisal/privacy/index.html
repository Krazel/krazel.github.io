This additional notice covers VORO for Android and Windows. The iOS sections above remain specific to the Apple edition.

Game progress, records where present and preferences are kept in local app storage; the game does not create an app account or send this gameplay data to a Krazel server. These editions contain no advertising, advertising SDK or independent analytics service. On Android, Microscope is free and the full game is unlocked with a single Google Play purchase. Windows retains its existing distribution model.

Android 1.2.0 (5) includes Google Play Billing 8.3.0. Alongside purchase processing, this library sends billing events and diagnostics to Google through DataTransport: app and library versions, billing-session identifiers, device and Android build information, language/region, time zone, and carrier/network metadata. These technical reports are separate from your local game progress and are not an advertising integration. Google processes store and SDK information under its policies; clearing game data does not control Google's retention. Google Play and its billing library use network connections for these functions.

The game receives product and purchase-state information, a purchase token, and the Google-signed receipt to verify access. It acknowledges completed purchases and refreshes ownership through Google Play. The verified receipt is kept in local app storage for offline access, not sent to a Krazel purchase server. Google handles payment-card information directly. Android system backups may include local app data when enabled, as described below.

VORO can use device motion locally when that control is selected. Android system backups can include local app data when enabled in your device settings; backup processing belongs to the operating-system service, not a Krazel game-data server.

Local saves remain until you reset them where the game offers that option, clear app data or remove its saved profile. Removing a Windows program may leave its local profile; ask support before deleting files if you want to keep progress.

Google Play handles Android distribution, payment, purchase restoration and refunds under its policies; the game does not receive payment-card details. The app verifies Google-signed purchase information and stores the purchase receipt locally to remember verified access when offline. It refreshes ownership through Google Play when available; no purchase receipt is sent to a Krazel server. Android and Apple purchases are separate and do not transfer between stores. A Windows distributor may separately process installation, purchase and account information. Opening public help pages or sending email uses external services: GitHub hosts these pages, and Gmail hosts the support mailbox. If you contact support, your email and message are used to handle the request; you can request deletion of that correspondence at coderappskrazel@gmail.com. Do not send credentials or device identifiers. See Google Privacy Policy / Política de privacidad de Google and GitHub privacy notice / aviso de privacidad de GitHub.
