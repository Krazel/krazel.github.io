The Daily Zodiac Support

The Daily Zodiac, shown as Zodiac Daily inside the app, is an iPhone app for reading and saving a beautifully presented daily horoscope card.

Common help

  • To change your zodiac sign, tap the current sign selector on the Today screen.
  • Tap the daily card to flip between its illustrated front and detailed reading.
  • Use Save Card to keep today's reading in your local collection.
  • If fresh content is temporarily unavailable, the app automatically shows an offline edition.
  • Your selected sign and saved cards are stored locally and do not require an account.

Optional supporter purchases

If supporter levels are available, prices and subscription terms are shown by Apple inside Settings. You can restore purchases in the app and manage or cancel a subscription from your Apple account settings. Core horoscope and saved-card features remain available without a subscription.
