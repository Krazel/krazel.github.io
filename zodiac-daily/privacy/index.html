The Daily Zodiac Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

The Daily Zodiac does not require an account and Krazel does not collect personal data through the app.

Data stored on your device

Your selected zodiac sign, saved cards, and app settings are stored only on your device and are not uploaded to Krazel.

Daily content request

To retrieve the daily edition, the app sends only the requested calendar date and the selected content language (English or Spanish) to Krazel's HTTPS service. It does not send your selected sign, birth date, saved cards, account information, advertising identifier, or precise location.

The hosting provider may process technical connection information, such as an IP address, only as needed to deliver and secure the request. Krazel does not retain this information or use it for identification, profiling, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Third parties

Krazel uses service providers only to deliver and secure the daily content request. They are required to protect information to the same or an equivalent standard. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not sell or share personal data for advertising or tracking.

Support messages

If you email support, Krazel uses your email address and message only to respond. They are retained only as long as needed for support or legal obligations.

Retention and deletion

Krazel does not retain personal data from the app. You can delete saved cards in the app; deleting the app removes all locally stored app data. You may request deletion of a support message using the contact address below, unless retention is legally required.

Contact

For privacy questions, email coderappskrazel@gmail.com.
