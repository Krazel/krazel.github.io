The Daily Zodiac Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

The Daily Zodiac, shown as Zodiac Daily inside the app, provides daily horoscope readings without requiring an account, birth date, or personal profile. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information stored on your device

Your selected zodiac sign, saved horoscope cards, supporter preferences, and app settings are stored locally on your device. Saved cards include a snapshot of their reading so they remain available later. This local information is not uploaded to a developer account or used to build a profile about you.

Daily horoscope requests

To retrieve the daily edition, the app sends the requested calendar date to a Krazel-operated HTTPS service hosted on Cloudflare. The app does not send your selected sign, birth date, saved cards, account information, advertising identifier, or precise location. The service returns a single edition containing readings for all twelve zodiac signs.

Cloudflare and ordinary internet infrastructure may process technical connection information, such as an IP address, for the time needed to route, secure, and deliver the request. Krazel does not use this information for advertising, tracking, analytics, or user profiling.

Horoscope content provider

The Krazel service retrieves horoscope content from FreeAstroAPI and caches the daily edition. The provider credential remains on the server and is never included in the iPhone app. The app does not contact FreeAstroAPI directly.

Purchases

If optional supporter purchases are offered, Apple processes them through the App Store. Krazel does not receive or store your payment card details. Purchase status is used only to show supporter status and related controls inside the app.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The Daily Zodiac does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. Krazel does not sell personal data.

Deleting local information

You can remove individual saved cards inside the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored sign, settings, cache, and saved cards from the device. App Store purchases can be managed from your Apple account settings.

Children

The Daily Zodiac is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Entertainment notice

Horoscope readings are provided for entertainment and personal reflection. They are not medical, financial, legal, or other professional advice.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app, its service providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
