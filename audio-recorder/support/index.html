Voice Recorder Pro - Audio K Support

Voice Recorder Pro - Audio K is an iOS app for recording, playing, and managing audio files locally on your device.

Common help

  • Make sure microphone permission is enabled in iOS Settings.
  • If recording does not start, close and reopen the app, then try again.
  • If sound-activated recording does not trigger, check the sensitivity setting and test in a quiet environment.
  • Recordings are stored locally unless you export or share them.
