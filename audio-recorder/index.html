Audio Recorder

A simple iPhone app for recording, organizing, playing, and sharing audio files stored on your device.

Main features

  • Continuous and sound-activated recording.
  • Adjustable audio quality, sensitivity, and file intervals.
  • Local playback, favorites, renaming, deletion, and sharing.
  • Seven interface languages: Catalan, German, English, Spanish, French, Italian, and Portuguese.
