Audio Recorder Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Audio Recorder is designed to record, play, and manage audio files locally on your device. This policy explains what data the app handles and how third-party services used in the app may process data.

Audio recordings

Your recordings are stored locally on your device unless you choose to export or share them using iOS sharing features. Audio Recorder does not upload your recordings to servers controlled by the developer.

App settings

Settings such as language, recording preferences, and app configuration are stored locally on your device and are used to provide app functionality.

Subscriptions and purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store your payment card details.

Advertising and consent

The app may use Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to show ads, request consent where required, and manage advertising preferences. Google Mobile Ads may process data such as approximate location derived from IP address, device identifiers, advertising data, product interaction data, crash data, and performance data. These practices are governed by Google's policies and the choices available in the consent prompt.

Data sharing

Audio Recorder does not sell your audio recordings. Third-party advertising services may process data as described above to provide ads, measure performance, prevent abuse, and comply with legal requirements.

Data deletion and control

You can delete recordings directly inside the app or by removing the app from your device. You can manage App Store subscriptions in your Apple account settings. Advertising consent choices can be changed where the app provides a privacy or consent option, or through your device privacy settings where applicable.

Children

Audio Recorder is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's features, advertising configuration, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the Audio Recorder support page.
