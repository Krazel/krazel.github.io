Quiet Stacks privacy policy

Quiet Stacks is made by Krazel Games, part of Krazel Studio. This policy covers the iPhone and iPad app.

The game works offline. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and does not automatically send us personal or gameplay data.

Progress, settings and any error information stay on your device. Deleting the app may erase progress; device backups are managed by Apple.

If you email support, we use your email address and message only to handle your request, and keep them only as needed for that purpose. Gmail hosts our mailbox. To ask a privacy question or request deletion of that correspondence, contact coderappskrazel@gmail.com.

These web pages are hosted by GitHub, which may process technical request data under its privacy policy. Apple handles App Store payments; we do not receive card details.
