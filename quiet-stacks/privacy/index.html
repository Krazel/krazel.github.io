Quiet Stacks privacy policy

Quiet Stacks is developed by Krazel Games, part of Krazel Studio. This policy describes the iPhone and iPad app.

No automatic data collection or tracking

The app does not automatically send gameplay, device information or personal data to us or to third-party services. It has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking, social features, or developer-operated cloud service. Artwork, book data, sound effects and music are bundled with the app and play offline.

Information stored on your device

Your book arrangement, camera position, story progress and audio settings are stored locally. The app can create local error diagnostics and optional performance reports containing timing measurements, app and operating-system versions, hardware model and technical performance information. Performance reports do not include your saved book layout. These records are not automatically uploaded.

Reports and support you choose to send

You can explicitly copy or share a report through the iOS share sheet, or email support. You decide whether to share it, which app to use, and who receives it. The destination service handles that information under its own policy. If you send us a support email, we receive your email address and the message or attachments you provide, and use them only to handle your request. Our support mailbox is hosted by Gmail. We retain support correspondence only as needed to resolve and follow up on the request; you can request deletion by email.

Your controls and deletion

You can adjust audio settings in the app. Local game data and diagnostic files are removed when you delete the app, subject to any device backups you manage with Apple. There is no developer account to delete and no server-side game profile. We cannot recover a lost local saved game.

Links and purchases

Opening Help & support or Privacy policy opens a page hosted on GitHub Pages in your browser. The website host may process standard request information under its own privacy policy. This is separate from offline gameplay. Apple handles the App Store purchase; the app does not receive your payment-card information.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion of support correspondence, email coderappskrazel@gmail.com. We will update this page if the app’s data practices change.
