Quiet Stacks support

Bring order to a warm, illustrated royal library. Quiet Stacks is a single-player sorting game for iPhone and iPad, played in landscape. The game and its artwork work offline.

Playing

  • Drag empty floor to move around. Pinch to zoom.
  • Tap a book to inspect its title, collection and volume. Drag it to move it.
  • Arrange volumes in the appropriate shelf positions. You can also leave books on open floor or tables.
  • The trolley has twelve positions. Drag onto an occupied trolley position to exchange the two books.
  • Your arrangement is saved automatically on this device. There are no accounts or developer-operated cloud saves.
  • Open Settings to adjust music and book sounds or reread Alden’s letter.

Help with a problem

If the game cannot open, use Reload gallery. If needed, you can copy the diagnostic shown by the app. Settings also includes an optional performance test and a report you can choose to share. Reports remain on your device unless you choose to send them.

For support or privacy questions, email coderappskrazel@gmail.com. Describe the problem and include your app version and iOS version. Never send passwords or other sensitive information.

Deleting the app removes its local data and may lose your progress. Read our privacy policy before sending a report.
